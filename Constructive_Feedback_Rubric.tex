| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Clarity of Message**     | Message is vague or confusing; unclear what the feedback is about. | Feedback is partially clear but lacks structure or examples.        | Feedback is understandable but somewhat wordy or imprecise.              | Feedback is clear and specific with some examples.     | Feedback is exceptionally clear, structured, and concise with strong supporting examples. |
| **Constructiveness**       | Feedback is overly negative, judgmental, or unhelpful.             | Attempts to be constructive but still focuses too much on problems. | Balances criticism and positives, but improvement suggestions are basic. | Offers balanced feedback with useful suggestions.      | Provides highly constructive, actionable feedback framed positively and thoughtfully.     |
| **Empathy & Tone**         | Tone is harsh, dismissive, or insensitive.                         | Shows minimal empathy; may sound forced or impersonal.              | Tone is respectful but lacks warmth or understanding.                    | Tone is considerate and shows genuine empathy.         | Tone is highly empathetic, encouraging, and supportive throughout.                        |
| **Specificity & Evidence** | Feedback is vague or based on assumptions.                         | Feedback includes minimal detail or examples.                       | Feedback references general behaviors but lacks depth.                   | Feedback includes relevant examples to support points. | Feedback uses clear, specific evidence that makes points credible and actionable.         |
| **Constructive Dialogue**  | Does not invite any response or discussion.                        | Barely acknowledges the other person’s perspective.                 | Acknowledges the other person but doesn’t build dialogue.                | Encourages a thoughtful exchange or reflection.        | Creates an open, two-way conversation that invites understanding and collaboration.       |
