| **Clarity of Decision**         | Avoids or delays stating that employment is ending.  | Mentions termination indirectly or confusingly. | States the decision but lacks clarity or uses overly soft language. | Communicates the decision clearly and professionally.                                        | Clearly and respectfully communicates the termination upfront, leaving no ambiguity.                          |
| **Reasoning & Accountability**  | Gives no explanation or blames the employee harshly. | Gives unclear or overly emotional reasoning.    | Provides a surface-level reason without full context.               | Explains the decision factually and fairly, referencing behavior or performance objectively. | Provides a transparent, well-structured explanation that connects to clear company standards or expectations. |
| **Empathy & Respect**           | Uses insensitive, cold, or robotic language.         | Attempts to sound kind but feels impersonal.    | Shows some empathy but lacks consistency or warmth.                 | Maintains a compassionate tone throughout the message.                                       | Shows deep empathy while maintaining professionalism and treating the employee with dignity.                  |
| **Next Steps & Transition**     | Provides no next steps or transition guidance.       | Gives vague or incomplete next steps.           | Outlines basic next steps without reassurance.                      | Clearly outlines offboarding process, logistics, and available support.                      | Clearly communicates offboarding details, benefits, and transition support in a calm and organized way.       |
| **Handling Emotional Response** | Ignores or dismisses the employee’s reaction.        | Acknowledges emotion but avoids addressing it.  | Responds politely but doesn’t validate feelings.                    | Responds with calm understanding and professionalism.                                        | Skillfully acknowledges emotion, maintains composure, and de-escalates while showing genuine care.            |
