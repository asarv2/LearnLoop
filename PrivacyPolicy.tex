\section{Introduction}
LearnLoop LLC (“LearnLoop,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website (\href{https://learn-loop.org}{https://learn-loop.org}), use our platform, or interact with our services (collectively, the “Service”).

By using LearnLoop, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our Service.

\section{Information We Collect}
We collect information in the following categories:

\subsection{1. Personal Information}
When you or your company create an account with LearnLoop, we may collect:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item Full name
    \item Email address
    \item Company name and role/title
    \item Account credentials
\end{itemize}

\subsection{2. Usage and Device Information}
We automatically collect certain information about your use of LearnLoop, including:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item IP address and browser type
    \item Device identifiers
    \item Log and usage data
    \item Cookies and analytics data
\end{itemize}

\subsection{3. User Content and Uploads}
During use of LearnLoop, you and your organization may upload or create:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item Audio or voice recordings
    \item Text entries, messages, or chat responses
    \item Uploaded documents or training materials
    \item Generated conversation transcripts and AI responses
\end{itemize}

\subsection{4. Payment and Billing Information}
For Clients under yearly contracts, payment information may be processed through our third-party payment processors. LearnLoop does not store or process credit card details directly.

\section{How We Use Your Information}
We use collected information to:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item Provide, operate, and maintain the LearnLoop platform
    \item Personalize and improve the training experience
    \item Facilitate account creation and company access management
    \item Provide technical support and respond to inquiries
    \item Analyze platform usage to enhance performance
    \item Comply with legal obligations
\end{itemize}

\section{Use of Artificial Intelligence (AI)}
LearnLoop uses AI systems (including APIs from OpenAI, Google Gemini, and Grok) to power communication simulations, provide feedback, and analyze user interactions.

Your text, voice data, and uploaded content may be processed by these AI systems to generate training responses. LearnLoop does not sell your data to third parties or use your data to train external AI models. All processing is conducted solely for the purpose of delivering and improving LearnLoop’s Service.

\section{Data Retention}
LearnLoop retains data indefinitely unless deletion is requested by the Client. This includes user profiles, content created during training, and uploaded materials. Clients may contact us to request deletion of data from their organization’s account.

\section{Cookies and Tracking}
We use cookies and similar technologies to:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item Maintain login sessions
    \item Measure website traffic and usage patterns
    \item Improve user experience and performance
\end{itemize}
You can adjust your browser settings to refuse cookies; however, this may limit some functionalities of the Service.

\section{How We Share Information}
We may share information in the following ways:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item With third-party service providers that help us operate our platform (e.g., hosting providers, AI APIs, analytics services, and payment processors)
    \item With your organization’s administrator for account management
    \item As required by law, regulation, or court order
    \item In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets
\end{itemize}

We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties.

\section{Data Security}
We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute data security.

\section{Your Rights and Choices}
Depending on your location, you may have rights to:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item Access, correct, or delete your personal data
    \item Restrict or object to processing
    \item Request data portability
    \item Withdraw consent for certain uses of your information
\end{itemize}

To exercise these rights, please contact us at \href{mailto:alex@learn-loop.org}{alex@learn-loop.org} or \href{mailto:ashok@learn-loop.org}{ashok@learn-loop.org}.

\section{International Data Transfers}
Although LearnLoop currently operates within the United States, data may be stored or processed by third-party services hosted globally. If LearnLoop expands internationally, we will ensure that all cross-border data transfers comply with applicable privacy laws such as the GDPR.

\section{Children’s Privacy}
LearnLoop is not directed toward individuals under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we learn that a child under 13 has provided personal information, we will take steps to delete it.

\section{Changes to This Policy}
LearnLoop may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of material changes by email or through notices on our website. The “Effective Date” at the top of this document will always reflect the latest version.
